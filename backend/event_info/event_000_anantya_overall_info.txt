OVERVIEW
========

Anantya is the premier annual national-level technical symposium organized by the Department of Computer Engineering at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Nigdi, Pune.

Anantya '26 brings together students, developers, designers, competitive programmers, cybersecurity enthusiasts, innovators, and creators from colleges and universities.

The symposium features a diverse set of technical and creative competitions covering competitive programming, cybersecurity, hackathons, AI/Web3, IoT and robotics, UI/UX design, and digital art.

Anantya '26 consists of 8 distinct events.

There are 8 events under Anantya '26:

==================================================
EVENTS BY DOMAIN
================

1. HACKATHONS AND BUILD COMPETITIONS

SHE SOLVES 3.0:
A multi-stage women-oriented hackathon focused on solving real-world problems through technology.

DECENTRAHACK 2.0:
A hackathon involving Web3, blockchain, agentic AI, and open-source technologies.

IoThrone 2026:
An innovation-focused hackathon involving IoT, AI/ML, computer vision, edge AI, robotics, and hardware/software prototyping.

2. COMPETITIVE PROGRAMMING

Codigo 2026:
A 3-round competitive programming contest involving DSA and competitive programming problems, with an ICPC-style offline final.

3. CYBERSECURITY

BYTE ME CTF '26:
A Capture The Flag cybersecurity competition involving areas such as web security, OSINT, forensics, cryptography, networking, Linux, and related security challenges.

4. UI/UX AND FRONTEND

MasterChef UI:
A UI/UX and frontend design competition involving interface design, user experience, visual design, and frontend-oriented challenges.

5. DIGITAL ART AND CREATIVE DESIGN

MAKE A DOODLE! 2026:
A digital doodling and creative-art competition involving digital illustration and theme-based artwork.

6. CAPSTONE AND SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE SHOWCASE

INNOVATE-X:
A capstone project presentation and system architecture competition for final-year B.Tech students organized by the Department of Computer Engineering in collaboration with CESA, ACM, ACM-W, OWASP, GDGC, and IRIS. Teams present their problem definition, system architecture, workflow, and tech stack.

PARTICIPATION
=============

Anantya includes events with different participation requirements.

Team sizes and eligibility depend on the individual event.

Examples:

* BYTE ME CTF '26: Teams of 1–2
* Codigo 2026: Exactly 3 members
* SHE SOLVES 3.0: Teams of 2–4 female participants
* DECENTRAHACK 2.0: Teams of 2–4
* MasterChef UI: Teams of 1–2
* IoThrone 2026: Teams of 2–4
* MAKE A DOODLE! 2026: Individual participation
* INNOVATE-X: Teams of up to 4 members (compulsory for all B.Tech final-year students)

IMPORTANT:
These rules are event-specific. The chatbot must use the relevant event file when answering questions about eligibility or team composition.

==================================================
CROSS-EVENT PARTICIPATION
=========================

Participants may participate in multiple Anantya events when permitted by the respective event rules and provided that their schedules do not conflict.

For team-based events, every member of the team must satisfy that event's eligibility requirements.

Do not assume that registration for one event automatically registers a participant for another event.
