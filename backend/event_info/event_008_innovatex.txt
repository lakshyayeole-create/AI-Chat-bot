[ELIGIBILITY & TEAM COMPOSITION]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Target Participants: All B.Tech final-year students (Participation is COMPULSORY for all final-year B.Tech students).
- Team Size: Maximum of 4 members per team.
- Team Changes: Alteration of team members is strictly NOT allowed once registered.
- Presence Requirement: All group members must be physically present during the presentation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[PROJECT REQUIREMENTS & SCOPE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Domain: Projects can be based on any domain—either strictly Computer Engineering or interdisciplinary.
- Deliverables: Participants must present their final project statement along with the detailed system architecture.
- Expected Preparation: Teams should be prepared to thoroughly explain:
  * Project Statement / Problem Definition
  * System Architecture & Design
  * Technical Workflow & Methodology
  * Technology Stack & Technical Choices made
  * Answers to questions posed by the panel of judges

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[EVENT FORMAT & TIMELINE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ROUND 1: PPT SUBMISSION & SHORTLISTING
- Date: 6 October 2026
- Mode: Online
- Process:
  * Teams must submit a presentation (PPT) containing their Final Project Statement and System Architecture.
  * Submitted PPTs will be evaluated by an expert judging panel based on predefined criteria.
  * Judges provide constructive feedback and suggest improvements.
  * Shortlisting: The top 20 teams will be shortlisted to advance to Round 2.

ROUND 2: FINAL REVIEW & PRESENTATION
- Date: 10 October 2026
- Mode: Offline (On-campus)
- Duration: 15 minutes per team (allocated for presentation, live demonstration, and Q&A).
- Process:
  * The 20 shortlisted teams present their refined project statement and system architecture in person before the final evaluation panel.
  * Teams demonstrate their solution and answer judge inquiries.
  * The top 3 projects are selected as the final winners.
