Overview:
Anantya is the premier annual national-level technical symposium organized by the Department of Computer Engineering at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Nigdi, Pune. Anantya brings together brilliant students, developers, designers, coders, ethical hackers, and innovators from colleges and universities across the country. It features a diverse lineup of high-impact technical and creative competitions, spanning competitive programming, cybersecurity, full-stack hackathons, AI & Web3, IoT & robotics, UI/UX design, and digital art.

REGISTRATION RULES & PRICING
==================================================

1. Pricing Structure:
   - PCCOE Students: Registration is 100% FREE (₹0) for all Anantya events. Valid PCCOE ID proof is required.
   - Non-PCCOE Students: A nominal entry fee is charged per team/individual (typically ₹100 per team for technical events, hackathons, and CTF; ₹50 to ₹100 for solo/art competitions).

2. Cross-Event Participation:
   - Yes, students can register and participate in multiple Anantya events as long as the offline round timings do not overlap.
   - For team events, all team members must meet the respective event's eligibility criteria (e.g. She Solves requires all female members; Codigo requires exactly 3 members).

3. Team Formation Rules:
   - BYTEME CTF '26: 1 to 2 members.
   - Codigo 2026: Strictly 3 members.
   - She Solves 3.0: 2 to 4 female members (all women).
   - DecentraHACK: 2 to 4 members.
   - MasterChef UI 2026: 1 to 2 members.
   - IoThrone 2026: 2 to 4 members.
   - Make a Doodle: 1 member (solo).
