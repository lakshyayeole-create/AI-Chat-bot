====================
EVENT OVERVIEW
==============

SHE SOLVES 3.0 is a multi-stage women-oriented hackathon organized by the PCCOE ACM-W Student Chapter.

The hackathon provides a platform for participants to innovate, build, and present technology-driven solutions to real-world problems.

The event focuses on the following domains:

* Health
* Environment
* Safety
* Education
* Entrepreneurship
* Women Empowerment

Event Theme:
Time Stone – Ideas Beyond Time

ROUNDS
======

ROUND 1 — REGISTRATION, IDEATION AND PROPOSAL SUBMISSION

Mode:
Online

Participants must submit:

* Team details
* Problem statement
* Proposed solution
* PPT

Deadline:
Up to 3rd October 2026

ROUND 2 — PROTOTYPE DEVELOPMENT & ONLINE DEMO

Mode:
Online

10 minutes

Date:
5th October 2026

ROUND 3 — OFFLINE HACKATHON

Mode:
Offline

Participants take part in a 5-hour offline hackathon.

During this round, teams build a solution based on an on-the-spot problem statement.

Date:
9th October 2026

Time:
9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Venue:
Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering, Pune
