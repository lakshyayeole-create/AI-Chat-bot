==================================================
EVENT OVERVIEW
==================================================

MasterChef UI is a competitive UI/UX design event that tests UI principles, observation, creativity, problem-solving, design execution and presentation.

The 2026 edition includes a strategy layer in which participants can earn XP through a pre-event campaign and convert XP into MasterChef Coins. Coins can be spent on permitted power-ups, defensive abilities, hints, strategic advantages and sabotages through the official event mechanism.

The competition consists of a Pre-Event Campaign followed by three official rounds:
- Round 1 — The Taste Test
- Round 2 — The Pressure Test
- Round 3 — The Signature Dish

Mode:

Participants explore a campaign website containing challenges, clues and hidden content to earn XP.

XP may be awarded for:

- Discovering hidden content
- Solving puzzles
- Finding Easter eggs
- Completing specific objectives
- Other activities announced by the organizers

The organizers determine the XP value of each activity. XP may contribute to a participant's or team's starting MasterChef Coin balance according to the announced conversion system.

==================================================
XP & MASTERCHEF COINS
==================================================

XP System:
Participants earn XP by completing designated Pre-Event Campaign activities. XP can be converted into MasterChef Coins according to the conversion system announced by the organizers.

MasterChef Coins:
MasterChef Coins are the official in-event currency and may be spent on:
- Power-ups
- Defensive abilities
- Hints
- Strategic advantages
- Permitted sabotages
- Mystery items
- Other mechanisms announced by the organizers

Important Rules:
- Coins do not determine the winner on their own.
- Coins cannot be bought with real money.
- Coins cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Coins cannot be transferred between teams.
- Coins cannot be gifted or traded between participants.
- Coins can only be used through the official event mechanism.
- Each team is responsible for managing its own coin balance.
- A confirmed transaction normally cannot be reversed.
- Unused coins have no monetary value after the competition.

==================================================
THE CHEF'S MARKET
==================================================

The Chef's Market is the official place where teams spend MasterChef Coins.

Power-Ups:
- Hint — provides an additional clue or information about a challenge.
- Time Boost — adds working time within organizer-defined limits.
- Reroll — allows a team to request a new eligible prompt, element or challenge component.
- Shield — protects the team from one eligible sabotage.
- Mystery Box — provides a randomly selected advantage.

Sabotages:
- Blackout — temporarily blocks a target team's access to a specified reference.
- Time Tax — reduces the target team's working time by a specified amount.
- Glitch — adds an extra design constraint for the target team.
- Swap — requires the target team to exchange a specified eligible element.
- Mystery Challenge — adds an extra challenge condition.

Sabotage Rules:
- Sabotages can only be activated during designated periods.
- Each sabotage has defined effects and limits.
- Teams receive a limited number of sabotage opportunities.
- Teams cannot repeatedly target the same team beyond organizer-defined limits.
- Sabotages cannot permanently prevent another team from completing a round.
- Sabotages must not delete, damage, access or modify another team's files.
- Organizers may cancel a sabotage that creates an unintended or unfair advantage.
