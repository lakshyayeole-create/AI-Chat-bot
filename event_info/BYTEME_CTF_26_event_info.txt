Overview:
BYTEME CTF is a Jeopardy-style Capture The Flag competition
where participants solve cybersecurity challenges, uncover hidden
flags, and submit valid flags through the official CTF platform.

The competition includes challenges across multiple cybersecurity
domains, including:
- Web
- OSINT
- Forensics
- Cryptography
- Networking
- Linux
- More cybersecurity domains

Participants earn points by successfully solving challenges.
Challenges may be solved in any order unless otherwise stated,
and the official scoreboard determines the final standings.

==================================================
TEAM & PARTICIPATION RULES
==================================================

- A participant may belong to only one registered team.
- Team composition is final after registration unless approved
  by the organizers.
- Participants must carry a valid student ID card.
- Participants must bring their own laptop and required
  accessories.
- Only registered team accounts and credentials may be used.


==================================================
ALLOWED ACTIVITIES
==================================================

- Collaboration is allowed only with members of your own
  registered team.
- Public documentation and technical resources may be used.
- Scripts, debuggers, disassemblers, packet-analysis tools,
  and other legitimate tools may be used.
- Technical clarifications and issues should be reported through
  the official BYTEME CTF communication channels.


==================================================
PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
==================================================

Participants must NOT:

- Share flags, complete solutions, challenge answers, or
  information that gives another team an unfair advantage.
- Share team accounts or credentials.
- Use another team's account.
- Attack the CTF platform or scoreboard.
- Attack the registration system or PCCOE infrastructure.
- Attack other participants.
- Perform denial-of-service attacks.
- Disrupt CTF services.
- Access, modify, delete, or damage another team's data or
  competition environment.
- Publish or distribute active flags or solutions before
  the competition ends.


==================================================
FINAL RULES
==================================================

- Clarifications, technical issues, and suspected unintended
  vulnerabilities must be reported through official BYTEME CTF
  communication channels.
- Communication channels must not be used to distribute flags
  or complete solutions during the competition.
- Participants or teams may be disqualified for rule violations,
  flag/solution sharing, unauthorized attacks, result manipulation,
  disruption, or bypassing competition controls.
- Flag sharing with another team may result in disqualification
  of the entire team and removal from the final standings.
- Organizers' decisions regarding rule enforcement, challenge
  validity, and competition integrity are final.
- Necessary schedule or competition changes will be communicated
  through official event channels.
- Registration and participation in BYTEME CTF constitute
  acceptance of these rules.
