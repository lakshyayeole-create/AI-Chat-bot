EVENT OVERVIEW:
Codigo 2026 is a 3-round competitive programming contest designed to assess participants' problem-solving skills, logical thinking, and proficiency in problem solving. The contest is designed to simulate the ICPC experience, with an online qualifier, an online semi-final, and a lab-based offline final. Participants solve progressively tougher DSA and competitive programming problems under contest conditions.

TEAM SIZE:
Exactly 3 members per team.

Individual participation is not permitted.

COMPETITION STRUCTURE:

ROUND 1:
Round Name: Qualifier Round
Platform: CodeChef
Mode: Online
Date: 6 October 2026
Duration: 2 Hours
Time: 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM
Problem Type: DSA-based questions
Selection: Top 45 teams will qualify for Round 2 based on contest performance.

ROUND 2:
Round Name: Semi-Final Round
Platform: CodeChef

Date: 7 October 2026

Round Name: Final Round

RULES AND REGULATIONS:

1. Team Composition:
   Participation is strictly allowed in teams of exactly three members. Individual participation is not permitted.

2. Team Participation:
   In every round, only the registered team as a unit must participate. Individual submissions by team members are not allowed.

3. Platform Compliance:
   All participants must follow the rules and code of conduct of the respective contest platforms, including CodeChef and HackerRank.

4. Fair Play:
   Any form of plagiarism, cheating, or use of unfair means will result in immediate disqualification of the team.

5. Organizer's Decision:
   The decision of the organizing committee of Anantya - Codigo 2026 is final and binding in matters related to the event.
