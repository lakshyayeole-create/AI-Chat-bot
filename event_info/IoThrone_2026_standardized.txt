==================================================
EVENT OVERVIEW
==================================================

IoThrone 2026 is an innovation-driven hackathon that encourages
participants to transform creative ideas into intelligent and
functional prototypes using technologies such as AI, ML,
Computer Vision, Edge AI, IoT, Robotics, and Hardware.

The event provides a hands-on platform to ideate, build, integrate,
and demonstrate technology-based solutions to real-world problems.

Participants go beyond just presenting an idea by developing a
working prototype and showcasing its capabilities in a practical
environment.

The hackathon follows a three-stage journey:

Concept → Prototype → Real-Time Integration

The three stages test participants' innovation, technical skills,
implementation, problem-solving, and ability to create practical
solutions.

EVENT FORMAT
==================================================

The hackathon consists of 3 rounds:

Round 1:
Online PPT Evaluation

Round 2:
Offline Prototype Demonstration

Round 3:
Offline Real-Time Implementation


==================================================
ROUND 1 — PPT EVALUATION
==================================================

Mode:
Online

Description:
Round 1 focuses on evaluating the team's idea, innovation, and
technical approach through a PowerPoint presentation.

The PPT must be submitted along with the event registration.

Teams should cover:
- Problem Statement – The real-world problem and its importance.
- Proposed Solution – How the solution addresses the problem.
- Innovation – Unique features and what makes the idea different.
- Technology Stack – AI/ML, IoT, hardware, software, sensors,
  and other technologies.
- System Architecture – Overall working flow and interaction
  between components.
- Implementation Plan – Steps involved in developing the prototype.
- Feasibility & Impact – Practicality, scalability, and expected
  real-world benefits.

Evaluation will focus on:
- Problem Understanding
- Innovation
- Technical Approach
- Feasibility
- Implementation Plan
- Real-World Applicability


==================================================
ROUND 2 — PROTOTYPE DEMONSTRATION
==================================================

Mode:
Offline

Description:
Round 2 focuses on evaluating the team's working prototype,

Selected teams will demonstrate their prototype before an internal

- Teams must use original ideas and work. Plagiarism or copied

  slot/timing.

- Teams must ensure their prototype is safe to operate and does not

- Any changes in schedule, venue, or round requirements will be
  communicated by the organizing team.
