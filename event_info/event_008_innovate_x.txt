EVENT OVERVIEW
==============

INNOVATE-X is a premier capstone project presentation and system architecture competition under Anantya '26, organized by the Department of Computer Engineering at PCCOE in collaboration with all major student bodies: CESA, ACM, ACM-W, OWASP, GDGC, and IRIS.

The event provides a platform for graduating B.Tech final-year students to present their final-year engineering projects, articulate their problem definitions, demonstrate comprehensive system architectures, justify technical choices, and defend their work before an expert evaluation panel.

==================================================
ELIGIBILITY AND TEAM COMPOSITION
================================
Target Participants: All B.Tech final-year students.
Participation Requirement: Participation is COMPULSORY for all final-year B.Tech students.
Team Size: Maximum of 4 members per team.
Team Changes: Alteration of team members is strictly NOT allowed once registered.
Presence Requirement: All group members must be physically present during the presentation and live evaluation.

==================================================
PROJECT REQUIREMENTS AND DOMAINS
================================
Domain Scope: Projects can be based on any domain—either strictly Computer Engineering or interdisciplinary.
Deliverables: Participants must present their final project statement along with the detailed system architecture.
Expected Technical Preparation:
* Project Statement and Problem Definition
* System Architecture and Architectural Design
* Technical Workflow and Methodology
* Technology Stack and justification of Technical Choices made
* Answers to technical and domain-specific questions posed by the expert panel of judges

==================================================
COMPETITION STRUCTURE AND TIMELINE
==================================

ROUND 1: PPT SUBMISSION AND SHORTLISTING
* Date: 6 October 2026
* Mode: Online
* Deliverable: Presentation (PPT) containing the Final Project Statement and System Architecture.
* Evaluation: Submitted PPTs evaluated by an expert judging panel based on predefined academic and technical criteria. Judges provide constructive feedback and suggest improvements.
* Shortlisting: Top 20 teams will be shortlisted to advance to Round 2.

ROUND 2: FINAL REVIEW AND PRESENTATION
* Date: 10 October 2026
* Mode: Offline (On-campus at PCCOE)
* Duration: 15 minutes per team (allocated for presentation, live demonstration, and Q&A).
* Process: The 20 shortlisted teams present their refined project statement and system architecture in person before the final evaluation panel, demonstrate their working solution, and answer judge inquiries.
* Winner Selection: The top 3 projects are selected as the final winners.
