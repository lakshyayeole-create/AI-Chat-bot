==================================================
EVENT OVERVIEW
==================================================

Make a Doodle is a creative digital art competition presented as
part of Anantya '26 by the Computer Department Art Circle, PCCOE.

The event gives participants a platform to turn ideas into digital
art and demonstrate creativity, visual storytelling, digital-art
skills, technical execution, and adaptability.

Participants create digital doodles using permitted creative tools
and respond to a live creative challenge in the final round.

Official message:
DON'T JUST DOODLE. DESIGN THE UNIMAGINABLE.

  students.
- Students from all recognized colleges/universities are eligible.

==================================================

- The submission must be the participant's own creative work.
- The artwork must be relevant to Marvel.
- The submission should demonstrate originality and creative

- Digital drawing/illustration tools may be used.
- AI tools may be used as part of the creative workflow.
- Late submissions may not be considered.
- Participants must provide accurate information while submitting
  their artwork.
- Any submission found to violate the competition guidelines may
  be disqualified.
