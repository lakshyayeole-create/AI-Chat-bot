EVENT OVERVIEW
==============

MAKE A DOODLE! is a digital doodling event conducted on computers as part of ANANTYA '26.

Participants turn their ideas into digital artwork using tools such as:

* Canva
* AI tools
* Their own creativity and digital art skills

The event consists of two rounds:

1. Round 1 — Online
2. Round 2 — Offline
