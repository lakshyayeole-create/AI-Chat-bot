# IoThrone 2026
Organized by: PCET’s Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering / IIRIS
Tagline: The Power Stone of Innovation! Think. Build. Integrate. Demonstrate.

## Description
Get ready to turn your innovative ideas into working prototypes at IoThrone 2026, an innovation-driven hackathon by PCET’s Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering.

## Domains
AI | ML | Computer Vision | Edge AI | IoT | Robotics | Hardware

## The Journey / Rounds
- Round 1 (PPT EVALUATION): Online. Registration & PPT submission closes: 27 September 2026. Selected teams move to Round 2.
- Round 2 (PROTOTYPE SHOWCASE): 9 October 2026 (9:00 AM – 4:00 PM) at 6206 & 6218 Labs, 2nd Floor, Computer Engineering Department, PCCoE. Showcase your working prototype before the judging panel. Top 15 teams advance.
- Round 3 (REAL-TIME INTEGRATION): 10 October 2026 (10:00 AM – 2:00 PM) at 6206 & 6218 Labs, 2nd Floor. Integrate your prototype with a provided/assigned environment or system and demonstrate its adaptability.

## Event Details
- Eligibility: Original and innovative project ideas
- Team Size: 2–4 members per team
- Hardware: Teams must arrange their required hardware/components.
- Prize Pool: ₹15,000+ Prize Pool + Goodies
- Perks: Exciting Goodies, ACM Chapter Membership
