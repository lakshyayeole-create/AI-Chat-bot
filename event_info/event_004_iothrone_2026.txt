====================
EVENT OVERVIEW
==============

IoThrone 2026 is an innovation-driven hackathon organized by IIRIS at PCET's Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering.

The hackathon encourages participants to transform creative ideas into intelligent and functional prototypes using technologies such as:

* Artificial Intelligence (AI)
* Machine Learning (ML)
* Computer Vision
* Edge AI
* Internet of Things (IoT)
* Robotics
* Hardware

The event provides a hands-on platform to ideate, build, integrate, and demonstrate technology-based solutions to real-world problems.

Participants are expected to go beyond presenting an idea by developing a working prototype and demonstrating its capabilities in a practical environment.

The official hackathon journey is:

CONCEPT → PROTOTYPE → REAL-TIME INTEGRATION

The overall workflow can also be described as:

IDEATE → BUILD → INTEGRATE → DEMONSTRATE

Participants must:

====================
ROUNDS
======

ROUND 1 — PPT EVALUATION

Mode:
Online

Round 1 focuses on evaluating the team's idea, innovation, and technical approach through a PowerPoint presentation.

PPT Submission:
The PPT must be submitted along with event registration.

Registration & PPT Submission Deadline:
27 September 2026

Teams selected in Round 1 advance to Round 2.

ROUND 1 — PPT CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

The PPT should cover:

1. Problem Statement
   The real-world problem being addressed and its importance.

2. Proposed Solution
   How the proposed solution addresses the problem.

3. Innovation
   Unique features and what makes the idea different.

4. Technology Stack
   Technologies used, including AI/ML, IoT, hardware, software, sensors, and other relevant technologies.

5. System Architecture
   Overall working flow and interaction between system components.

6. Implementation Plan
   Steps involved in developing the prototype.

7. Feasibility & Impact
   Practicality, scalability, and expected real-world benefits.

ROUND 1 — EVALUATION CRITERIA

Evaluation focuses on:

* Problem Understanding
* Innovation
* Technical Approach
* Feasibility
* Implementation Plan
* Real-World Applicability
