==================================================
EVENT OVERVIEW
==================================================

DecentraHACK — Marvel Edition is a 3-round hackathon for students who
want to build what actually matters.

Across three rounds, teams take an idea from an abstract, to a working
demo, to a live pitch in front of the judges.

It is organized around three themes:
- Agentic AI
- Blockchain / Web3
- Open Source

Teams are free to pick any problem within these themes and use any
tools they like, including AI tools.

Official tagline/message:
A small idea can open a bigger universe.
Same universe. Brighter ideas.


==================================================
ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

- College students in second year and above are eligible.
- Cross-branch teams are allowed.
- All members of a team must belong to the same college.
- Cross-college teams are not allowed.

- Solo participation is not allowed.

EVENT FORMAT
==================================================

The hackathon consists of 3 rounds:

Round 1:
Idea Submission — a written abstract

Round 2:
Development Phase — working demo submission + PPT 

Round 3:
Final Presentation — offline pitch to judges

FAQ / DIRECT ANSWERS

Q: What is DecentraHACK?
A: DecentraHACK — Marvel Edition is a 3-round hackathon that takes
teams from an idea, to a working demo, to a live pitch in front of
judges, built around Agentic AI, Blockchain/Web3 and Open Source.

Q: Who can participate?
A: College students in second year and above. Cross-branch teams are
allowed, but all team members must be from the same college.

Q: How many members can be in a team?
A: Teams must have a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 members. Solo
participation is not allowed.

Q: What are the three rounds?
A: Round 1 is idea submission (a written abstract), Round 2 is the
development phase (working demo), and Round 3 is the final in-person
presentation.

Q: What is the Round 1 submission?
A: A 500-word abstract covering the idea, the problem and its
real-world impact, feasibility, novelty, architecture, and revenue
model.

Q: When do Round 1 and Round 2 deadlines fall?
A: They are not fixed in the rulebook; they will be shared with
qualified teams in the official participant groups.

Q: What does Round 2 require?
A: A working demo submitted as a video via a Google Drive link, along
with a presentation. The link must be accessible before submission.

Q: When and where is the final round?
A: The final round is on 9 October 2026 at the PCCOE campus, Pune.
The top 15 teams pitch in person to the judges.

Q: What themes can participants choose from?
A: Agentic AI, Blockchain/Web3, and Open Source. Teams may pick any
problem within these themes and use any tools, including AI tools.

Q: When do registrations open and close?
A: Registrations open on 20 September 2026 and close on 30 September
2026.

Q: What is the registration fee?
A: Not specified in the supplied rulebook.

Q: What is the prize pool?
A: The prize pool is ₹20,000+ ("₹20K+").

Q: Do participants receive certificates?
A: Yes, certificates are given to all participants. Goodies and
memberships go to top winners.

Q: What happens if a team violates the rules?
A: Plagiarism or misconduct can lead to immediate disqualification.
Only the idea submitted in Round 1 may be developed further, and all
work must be done during the hackathon period.

Q: Who makes the final decision?
A: The decisions of the organizing committee and judges are final.
