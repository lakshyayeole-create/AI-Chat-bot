Overview:
She Solves 3.0 is a multi-stage women-oriented hackathon that
provides a platform for participants to innovate, build and present
technology-driven solutions to real-world problems.

The hackathon focuses on:
- Innovation and Creativity
- Technical Skills
- Problem Solving
- Practical Implementation
- Social Impact

Official tagline/message:
Think beyond limits. Build beyond time.
Don't just participate. Innovate. Build. Solve.

Requirement:

==================================================
ROUND 1 — IDEA SUBMISSION
==================================================

Round Name:
Registration, Ideation and Proposal Submission

Mode:
Online

Deadline:
Up to 3 October 2026

Description:
Teams must complete their registration and submit a detailed proposal
explaining their chosen problem, proposed solution, and technical
approach.

Submission Requirements:
- Team details
- Problem statement
- Proposed solution
- Technology/domain
- Expected impact
- PowerPoint presentation

PPT Format:
She Solves PPT Template

Evaluation Criteria:
- Innovation
- Feasibility
- Clarity
- Relevance
- Potential impact

Selection:
Top teams will be shortlisted for Round 2.

Important:
All submissions must be completed on or before the specified deadline.


==================================================
ROUND 2 — PROTOTYPE DEVELOPMENT
==================================================

Round Name:
Prototype Development and Online Demo

Mode:
Online

Date:
5 October 2026

Description:
Shortlisted teams will develop a working prototype/MVP based on their
proposed solution.

Submission Requirements:
- Working prototype / MVP
- Project / GitHub link
- Required submission details

Online Demo:
Each team will get 10 minutes to explain and demonstrate its prototype.

Evaluation Criteria:
- Functionality
- Technical implementation
- Innovation
- Feasibility
- Clarity of presentation

Selection:
Selected teams will advance to Round 3.


==================================================
ROUND 3 — OFFLINE HACKATHON
==================================================

Round Name:
Offline Hackathon

Mode:
Offline

Date:
9 October 2026

Time:
9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Location:
Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering, Pune

Duration:
5 hours (as stated in the rulebook)

Description:
Finalist teams will participate in an offline hackathon.

Teams will be provided with an on-the-spot problem statement and will
develop a solution within the given time.

Teams must:
- Build their solution
- Test their solution
- Refine their solution
- Submit the completed work before the end of the round


==================================================
JUDGING CRITERIA
==================================================

Teams are evaluated based on:

- Innovation and Creativity
- Technical Implementation
- Functionality
- Feasibility
- Impact
- Presentation and Clarity


==================================================
WINNER ANNOUNCEMENT
==================================================

Winners will be announced after the final evaluation based on overall
performance.


==================================================
GENERAL RULES & GUIDELINES
==================================================

- Teams must strictly adhere to all deadlines and submission guidelines.
- Any form of plagiarism, misconduct, or rule violation will result

- All team members are expected to actively contribute throughout
