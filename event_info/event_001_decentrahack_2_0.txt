====================
EVENT OVERVIEW
==============

DECENTRAHACK 2.0 is a 3-round technical hackathon organized by the LFDT Student Chapter at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE).

The hackathon focuses on ideation, building, demonstration, and pitching solutions across technical tracks including:

* Agentic AI
* Blockchain / Web3
* Open Source

The event follows a three-stage progression:

1. IDEA — Submit an abstract
2. BUILD — Develop and present a working demo with PPT
3. PITCH — Top 15 teams pitch their solutions at PCCOE

====================
